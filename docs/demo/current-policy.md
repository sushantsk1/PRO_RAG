# Northstar Devices support policy

Document version: 2026-09. Applies to purchases made on or after 2026-09-01.

## Warranty

Northstar Devices provides a 24-month limited hardware warranty from the purchase date for manufacturing defects. A customer must keep the receipt and device serial number. Accidental damage and water damage are excluded.

## Returns

An unopened device may be returned within 30 days of delivery. An opened device may be returned within 14 days if it is defective on arrival. Shipping charges are not refunded.

## Support hours

Live support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time. Customers can submit a support ticket at any hour; tickets are reviewed on the next business day.
