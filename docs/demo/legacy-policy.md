# Northstar Devices legacy support policy

Document version: 2024-01. Applies to purchases made before 2026-09-01.

## Warranty

The legacy limited hardware warranty lasts 12 months from the purchase date. It covers manufacturing defects and excludes accidental damage. The current policy for newer purchases may differ.

## Returns

The legacy return window for unopened devices is 21 days from delivery. A customer must present a receipt.
